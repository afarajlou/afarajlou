### Research notebook: learning by measurement

I organize new explorations into **four-day mini-sprints**:

1. **Question & reading:** identify a bottleneck or architecture trade-off and study a reference.
2. **Model & implement:** build a small, correct baseline, including CPU-based simulations where hardware is unavailable.
3. **Measure & analyze:** evaluate assumptions, sensitivity, performance, and limitations.
4. **Document & reflect:** publish reproducible results and open questions.

**Planned areas:** HPC performance modeling · AI accelerators · LLM systems · CUDA/GPU concepts · memory architectures · neuromorphic computing · SNNs.

> **Research integrity:** I distinguish measured hardware results from simulations and analytical predictions. Experiments, results, and source references will be published as they are validated—not before.
